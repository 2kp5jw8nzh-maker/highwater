Theranos: How a $9 Billion Startup’s Technology Never Actually Worked

Theranos was valued at nine billion dollars in 2014, built on a machine that promised to run hundreds of medical tests from a single drop of blood. The machine never actually worked, and by 2018, the company was worth nothing. We break down the boardroom full of credibility and no medical expertise, the Wall Street Journal investigation that broke it open, and the fraud conviction that sent its founder to federal prison for eleven years.

Blockbuster: How Turning Down a $50 Million Offer Led to Bankruptcy

In 2000, Netflix’s founders offered to sell their company to Blockbuster for fifty million dollars. Blockbuster said no. Ten years later, Blockbuster filed for bankruptcy with nine hundred million dollars in debt — and the company it turned down was worth billions. We break down the deal Blockbuster walked away from, the late-fee backlash that followed it, and the second chance at online rental it also let slip away.
